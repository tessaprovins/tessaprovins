# Tessa Provins

Assistant Professor, [School of Government & Public Policy](https://sgpp.arizona.edu/), University of Arizona

I study how institutional rules shape who is represented and what policy gets made, in state legislatures, in Congress and in tribal governments.

**Research areas:** Legislative institutions · Indigenous politics & policy · Voting access & representation · State policy
